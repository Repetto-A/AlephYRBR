## Executive summary

- **WDK** rewards meaningful wallet/payment infrastructure: either CLI/MCP-based agentic payments or truly gasless UX.
- **Pears** rewards a real, installable standalone CLI distributed by `pear://`, with working P2P OTA updates. The deployment loop is the product requirement, not decoration.
- **QVAC** rewards private local AI that either automates operations work or demonstrates measurable reliability on hard tool-use tasks.
- **General** is technology-agnostic and rewards execution and impact, but its $500 pool is smaller and its brief is less differentiated.

## Important selection constraint

Each Tether page says a participant can enter **one track from this sponsor**. Treat that as meaning you must choose among WDK, Pears, and QVAC unless organizers confirm otherwise. General is listed under Crecimiento, so cross-entry may be possible, but verify this before submission.
