# General Track — research brief

## One-line thesis

Build the strongest high-impact project of the weekend, regardless of whether it is crypto, Web3, AI, robotics, or another category.

## Prize structure

- Total: **$500 USDC**
- 1st: **$300 USDC**
- 2nd: **$200 USDC**

## Brief

The track is deliberately open-ended. It rewards:

- Strong execution.
- High-impact problem solving.
- A complete, practical, clearly presented result.

There are no specialized technology requirements on the published track page.

## Strategic interpretation

- Best fit for a project whose core advantage does not naturally depend on WDK, Pears, or QVAC.
- The broad brief creates more freedom but less sponsor-specific differentiation.
- Because the prize pool is smaller, use it as the primary target only if the specialized tracks would force a cosmetic integration.
- Since General belongs to Crecimiento rather than Tether, a specialized-track project may possibly enter General too; confirm the submission policy with organizers.
