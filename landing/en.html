The differentiator

The only assistant that knows beforehand

Documentation assistants write down what already happened. Prognosia also reads the chart and, before the note is signed, checks that the plan does not contradict it. That is why we say it knows beforehand: it catches the error the moment it is made, not once it is already in the record.

Seguridad WITHHELD

1 · Cross-checks

It compares every order in the plan against the patient's history, allergies and current medication.

2 · Withholds

If there is a contraindication, the order is withheld and the note cannot be signed until the plan is fixed.

3 · Explains

It shows you the evidence: what the chart says, what was said in the visit, and why they clash. No black boxes.
